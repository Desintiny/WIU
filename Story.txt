======Story 1======
You lived in a small town, named Havenbrook, it was peaceful and buzzing with life. 
Until the peace was disturbed, sirens blared, The Blackthorn Empire invaded the town. 
The ruthless enemies had killed all you loved, but you managed to escape.
Planting a deep seed of hatred, you vowed to take back what you own and avenge your loved ones

======Story 2======
You scrape together, whatever you can do to survive. 
You made a makeshift weapon, it is only enough to help you temporarily. 
Right now, you are hiding in a bush from the incoming guards of the Blackthorn Empire. 

======Story 3======
You have slain all in your way, the forest is yours to conquer but fate will play tricks on you. 
After the gruesome obstacles you faced so far, you finally stood in front of the gate of the village 
you once called your home.

======Story 4======
You feel a sense of familiarity and hope, but the village you pictured once is in rubble. 
You cannot help but shed a tear from the overwhelming events that happened so quickly. 

======Story 5======
You reflect on your progression since you were outside the gates, you feel uneasy, despite having done so much. 
But you cannot stop here, you have done too much to give up, so you continued heading forward.

======Story 6======
You are nearing the gate where the king rests, his throne sat by an imposter. 
Only you know that you can save HavenBrook, the only one who can avenge your loved ones. 
Feeling motivated, you continue fighting.

======Story 7======
You feel a sense of dread but you know this is the only obstacle left. 
You cannot step back now, the only direction is to head on.
